Thursday, 9:47. A client meeting at 10:30. A site visit at the same time. And in email, a new quote buried under an old one.

Old quote: $16,900. New quote: $18,400. Difference: $1,500 for reclaimed wood counter. The subject line even said “FINAL (v3) — actually final.”

This page is not about a code. It is about the morning check that caught that before it was sent — and how to set one up yourself, without missing the 48-hour window if you join Muse.

Who this is for

If your day looks like this, this workflow is for you:

  • You open email and the important thing is not at the top.
  • You are double-booked, and you only find out when it is too late.
  • You have already sent a version, then found a newer version after.

If none of that happens to you, you do not need this. Close the page.

The morning check, in 3 concrete steps

Step 1 — What is due today? Muse scans email and calendar for today’s deadlines and meetings, and lists only today, not everything.

Step 2 — What clashes? It flags double-bookings in plain words, for example: client meeting 10:30 and site visit 10:30 at the same time.

Step 3 — What is buried? It pulls the newest version of a quote, contract, or task out of a long thread, and puts old vs new side by side.

Then you check it yourself. In the $1,500 example, Maya checked the new price, edited two numbers, and sent it herself. Muse did not send it for her. That check is the whole point — an agent that sends without you checking is a liability, not help.

Set it up yourself: a checklist you can copy

  1. Connect email and calendar first. Nothing else matters until those two are in.
  2. Ask for one page, every morning, before you open email: due today, clashes, buried items.
  3. Make it a scheduled task, same time every morning, not a one-off chat.
  4. Keep approvals on for sending mail, booking, and paying. Always.
  5. Friday review: what slipped this week, what to carry to next week. One page, same format.

What Muse will not do for you

  • It will not know which quote is right if you do not check. It finds the newest, you decide.
  • It will not fix a bad client relationship, a bad price, or a missed deadline you never told it about.
  • It will not replace your judgment on sending, booking, or paying. Keep that approval on.
